How the signals work

Our indicator watches Bitcoin and Ethereum on the daily and multi-day charts. It looks for one specific moment: when selling pressure has run its course and buyers start taking back control.

A signal never fires on a guess. The setup has to form, then hold through a few candle closes before it confirms. By the time you get an alert, the signal is final and can't vanish.

Signals only fire while our market filter says the broader trend is on your side. In a downtrend the system stays quiet, sometimes for a year.

Every confirmed setup is checked against several independent confirmations. Only the ones where the most line up reach members.

The odds on each card come from how past signals with the same profile behaved over the next 60 days. They measure the quality of the entry, not a trading result.

Reading a card
The big number
How many past signals like this one rose 10% within 60 days of the entry close. The line under it: how many did that without ever falling 25%, the dip that would wipe out a 4x position.
Typical high
The median best price within 60 days, from the entry close.
Typical drop
The median deepest dip within 60 days. Size your position and leverage so this dip doesn't hurt you.
Where the odds fade
This setup's own record: after it first falls 5%, 10%, 15% or 20%, how many of its past signals still rose 10%. The number of signals is shown on every row.
Edge gone
The first fall at which this setup's odds drop below our 70% bar.
If it misses
Of the past signals that did not reach +20%, how many were above entry first, and their median peak before they failed.

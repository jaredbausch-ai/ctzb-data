How the signals work

Our indicator watches Bitcoin and Ethereum on the daily and multi-day charts. It looks for one specific moment: when selling pressure has run its course and buyers start taking back control.

A signal never fires on a guess. The setup has to form, then hold through a few candle closes before it confirms. A confirmed setup is final and can't vanish. A forming setup can: it is shown before the confirming close.

A signal only counts if our market filter said the broader trend was on your side on the day the bar that made its low opened (on a 2D, 3D or 5D chart that can be a few days before the low itself). In a downtrend the system stays quiet, sometimes for a year. The header shows the filter as it is today, so a setup whose low came just before the filter turned off can still appear while the header reads off.

Every confirmed setup is checked against several independent confirmations. Only the ones where the most line up reach the top grade.

The odds on each card come from how past signals with the same profile behaved over the next 60 days. They measure the quality of the entry, not a trading result.

Where the odds fade

Edge gone
The first fall at which this setup's odds drop below our posting bar.
If it misses

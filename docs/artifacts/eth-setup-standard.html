What this is, and is not

Computed signals, not trades. No position is represented here.

No exit rule is in any number. "+20%" means price touched it, not that it was sold.

The median drawdown is the middle, not the floor. Half of past setups dipped deeper. The line under the verdict gives the share of past setups of this grade whose dip reached your liquidation point.

The regime filter has a known blind spot. It stayed on through the November 2021 top.

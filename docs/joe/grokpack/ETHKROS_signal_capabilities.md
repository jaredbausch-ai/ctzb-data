# ETHKROS signal capabilities

Prepared 24 Oct 2026 by Jared Bausch for Mauro Group's AI. Every figure was recomputed on 24 Oct from the full price history with the rules below. Earlier figures in emails and in the October odds pages are superseded by this document.

## How to read every number here

- **Entry quality only.** These are historical results of computed signals. No position has been taken on any of them, and there is no exit in the data, so nothing here is a return, a track record, or a member win rate. Live results will come from Jared's own logged trades.
- **Win** = price traded at least **+20% above the entry** at some point in the **60 days** after entry (daily highs). Entry is the close of the bar where the signal confirms, which is when the alert fires. Nothing is counted before it was visible.
- **Regime on.** A Bitcoin-driven filter switches signals on and off. Every figure is measured with it on.
- **Two periods, always.** 2016–21 and 2022–26 are shown separately. 2022–26 rates are lower mainly because the market moves less (ETH's daily volatility fell from 5.7% to 3.6%), so a fixed +20% is a higher bar.
- **Drawdown** = the worst dip below entry before the 60-day high (median). **Touched −30%** = share that dipped 30% or more below entry within 60 days.
- **n** = number of past signals. Under about 20 is a small sample. Many setups were tested, so some high figures will not repeat.

## 5. What each live alert can carry

- Chart, tags, entry price and time.
- The matching rows above: win rate, both periods, drawdown, touch rates, n.
- Which other charts or the other asset already confirmed in the previous 10 days.
- Whether the higher timeframe is rising, and whether the regime is on.
- After entry: the day-5 and day-10 reads in section 4.
- Jared's own buy and sell calls, with his leverage, by membership tier. Members choose their own leverage; the leverage tool shows where their liquidation would sit against the drawdowns above.

HOW TO READ THIS

What changed in v3. The EDGE column was recomputed. A chart’s baseline IS its all-troughs win rate, so an ALL TROUGHS row must show an edge of zero by definition. In v2 the ETH 5D ALL TROUGHS row showed 84% win and +14 edge, which was the tell: the 84% was the baseline. Inside a bull window every trough on the ETH 5D went 84% at 60 days, so no tag on that chart beats simply taking them all. ETH 5D “Any diamond” went from +14 to −1. ETH 3D, BTC 1D and BTC 2D shifted by 2 to 3 points. ETH 1D and ETH 2D are unchanged.
THE COLUMNS
UPSIDEMedian best price reached by that horizon. We take profit, so this is what was available — not the close at day 60.
RISKThe worst close between entry and the eventual peak. The most you were ever underwater before the trade worked. Measured on closes, so exchange wicks are excluded.
WORSTThe single worst instance of RISK in that cell. One trade, not a median.
EDGEPercentage points of win rate over that chart’s own bull-window all-troughs rate. Zero means the tag told you nothing the chart did not.
GAINConfluence only. The lift over the SAME tag on the SAME chart without the confirmer.
WHAT TO TRUST
The cycle filterThe primary edge. Everything here is measured inside bull windows anchored to Bitcoin all-time highs, Window 4 from 2026-06-24. Outside those windows the tags are close to worthless.
Diamonds on the ETH 2D89% at 60 days against a 69% baseline on 35 signals, 4.6 a year, risk −2.0%. Validated across RSI lengths 8 to 28 and pivot widths 2 to 5, so it is not fitted to the defaults. The single best cell in the study.
The ETH 5D as a CONFIRMERNot as a chart to trade. A signal on one of the other five charts within 10 days of an ETH 5D trough went 82% with +41.3% upside, against 63% and +23.6% outside that window — +18pp on n=1064. The strongest single effect in the stack, and it survived re-measurement. The 5D’s own tags add nothing; its value is entirely as a clock for the other charts.
The prime dotStoch K between 20 and 50 at the trough. Pooled over nine charts, diamonds with the dot went 79% and +39.6% upside against 71% and +29.1% without. Positive at 10, 20, 30, 60 and 90 days and on 8 of 9 charts. XRP is the exception.
Bucket AStrongest bucket on BTC and on the ETH 5D.

WHAT NOT TO TRUST

The top of the confluence table560 combinations were computed and the page is sorted by the best of them. That is precisely how a false positive is manufactured. An earlier confluence finding on this data looked significant at p=0.003 and dissolved to p=0.42 when a longer file was supplied.
Anything under about 15 signalsDirectional at best. Counts under 10 (solo) and 12 (confluence) are printed in red. Several cells rest on 3 to 8 trades.
Overlapping measurementSignals on the 1D and 2D of one asset often describe the same market move, and the 60-day windows of consecutive signals overlap heavily. The effective sample is far smaller than the counts suggest.
GOLD over HOLLOWThe filled gold diamond is not the stronger glyph. Pooled over eight charts, excluding XRP, the HOLLOW diamond went 81% with +42.5% upside against the filled diamond’s 72% and +31.4%. Hollow beat filled on 7 of 9 charts. XRP is the sole reversal. The colouring on the chart implies a ranking the data does not support.
The bucket boundariesMove 40/55/65 by five points and which letter looks worst changes. A/B/C/D is vocabulary, not measurement. Bucket C is weakest at the current boundaries on most charts, but that is a property of where the lines sit.
Filters that were tested and droppedRange expansion, volume, money flow, stochastic slope, stochastic level and the WGMI liquidity star all came back null or worse. The range expansion filter discarded a fifth of all diamonds and the discarded group had HIGHER upside (+38.5% vs +31.9%) and LOWER risk than the group it kept. It was removed from the indicator and the scanner in v9 / v6.
IntradayThe diamond carries no information below the daily. On ETH 4H and 12H it scores −3 to +2pp at every horizon.

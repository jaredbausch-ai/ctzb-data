What each quality level has done

The worst-on-record column is the one that sets your limit. Meeting all three checks has never produced a drawdown past −27.1%; everything below it has gone past −43%.

The standard — three checks
01
The low carries a marker
A diamond, a prime dot, or both. Without one it is an ordinary bounce.
02
It sits higher than the previous signal
A series of lows stepping down is a falling market producing repeated signals. Those are the ones that fail.
03
Two other timeframes already agreed
At least two other charts printed their own low in the preceding ten days — counted backwards only, so it is known on the day.
What this is, and is not

These are computed signals, not trades. Nothing here represents a position taken or money put at risk. It is what the indicator would have flagged, measured against what price then did.

No exit rule is in any of these numbers. "Reached +20%" means price touched that level, not that it was sold there. Every figure is an opportunity and its cost, never a realised return.

Worst-on-record is not worst-possible. The console sizes against the deepest drawdown in ten years of history. A future one can be deeper. Leave margin; do not size to the line.

The samples are small. The full standard rests on 58 past setups. Three or four different outcomes move the 81% by several points.

The regime filter has a known blind spot. It stayed on through the November 2021 cycle top, and setups firing in that window went 1 for 35. A green regime is not evidence that a top is not forming.

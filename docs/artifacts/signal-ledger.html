Every graded CTZB signal on ETH and BTC, 1D to 5D, one row each: its grade, the lenses that were on, the grade’s odds, and what happened in the 60 days after entry. ETH is graded from 2020 (funding data), BTC from 2016.

Grade odds are each grade’s record over the whole sample, including the signal itself, so for past rows they are hindsight, not what was knowable on the day. Odds on the day are the same grade’s rates using only signals whose 60 days had finished before that entry, so they are what the grade would have quoted at the time; blank until 10 such signals existed. Drawdowns are from the entry close (the confirmation bar). Best move is the highest high within 60 days, the best possible exit, not a return. Entry quality only: these are computed signals, never fills, and there is no exit in the data, so nothing here is a strategy return or a track record. Data through 28 Oct 2026; signals still inside their 60 days are not listed.

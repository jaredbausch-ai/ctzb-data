WHAT THE VALIDATION FOUND

The indicator passed both tests. Every setting was frozen on data up to 31 Dec 2021 and scored on 2022–2026 untouched. The diamond edge went from +1pp in-sample to +11pp out. The ETH 5D window went from +14pp to +39pp out. The ETH 2D diamond went from 71% to 80%. Nothing was re-tuned. Separately, the hand-drawn bull windows were replaced with a rule that could have been followed live, and the edge survived — it did not depend on hindsight.
But the absolute win rates were inflated, and this sheet now says so. The old windows ended on known cycle tops. They excluded exactly the stretches that failed, which lifted every baseline by roughly nine points. ETH 2D diamonds read 89% under those windows and 75% under a live rule — same signals, honest measurement. Plan with the white columns.
THE REGIME RULE
How it worksDriven by BTC 2D CT RSI. BULL after 8 consecutive bars at or above 60. BEAR after 12 consecutive bars at or below 40. In between, the state holds — so an ordinary correction passes through the middle without flipping you.
Why 8 bars for bullEvery run of BTC 2D CT RSI at or above 60 was sorted by regime: runs of 8+ bars happened 27 times inside bull windows and zero times in a bear period. The longest a bear ever held the zone was 7 bars. ETH 2D is the same. The test uses no return data, so the threshold is not fitted to P&L.
Why the bear side is slowerThe mirror test does not separate. Runs of CT RSI at or below 40: 3+ bars is 20 bull / 16 bear, 8+ bars is 4 / 8, 12+ bars is 2 / 6. Bull markets dip into the bear zone constantly, so a brief visit means nothing. 12 bars is the shortest length where bear periods clearly dominate.
Why not combine with the halvingTested. Requiring both gave 89% win and +103.8% upside in-sample and 50% on 20 signals out of sample. Textbook overfitting. One rule or the other, never both.

RISKThe worst close between entry and the eventual peak. Measured on closes, so exchange wicks are excluded.

WHAT TO TRUST
The regime filterThe primary edge, and it is not hindsight. Pooled diamonds score +9 edge under a live rule and +1 with no regime filter at all. That last number is the most important in the study: strip the filter and the diamond is worth nothing.
Diamonds on the ETH 2D75% live on 36 signals, +46.5% upside, risk −2.3%. Validated across RSI lengths 8 to 28 and pivot widths 2 to 5, so not fitted to the defaults. Still the best cell in the study.
The ETH 5D as a CONFIRMERNot as a chart to trade. A signal on another chart within 10 days of an ETH 5D trough lifted the win rate +39pp out of sample. It also holds with no regime filter at all, so it does not depend on the cycle call. The strongest and most robust effect in the stack.
The prime dotStoch K between 20 and 50 at the trough. Flat in-sample, +16pp out of sample on ETH and BTC diamonds with upside more than doubled. Promoted to its own glyph and alert in v11.

WHAT NOT TO TRUST

The old win ratesEvery figure in the grey columns assumed windows that ended on known cycle tops. Roughly nine points of that was hindsight.
The top of any sorted table539 confluence combinations were computed and the page is sorted by the best. That is how a false positive is manufactured. Worse: the #1 and #2 tags on the 2021 leaderboard were the worst performers afterwards, while the ETH 2D diamond ranked only 4th. Trade the structural effects, not the top row.
Anything under about 15 signalsDirectional at best. Counts under 10 (solo) and 12 (confluence) are in red. Several cells rest on 3 to 8 trades.
Overlapping measurementSignals on the 1D and 2D of one asset often describe the same move, and consecutive 60-day windows overlap heavily. The effective sample is smaller than the counts suggest.
GOLD versus HOLLOWEarlier versions of this sheet said the hollow diamond was the stronger glyph. That failed out of sample — hollow led by +10pp before 2022 and trailed by 1pp after. Neither glyph reliably leads. The claim has been removed.
The bucket boundariesMove 40/55/65 by five points and which letter looks worst changes. A/B/C/D is vocabulary, not measurement. Bucket C is weakest at the current boundaries — 59% out of sample — but that is a property of where the lines sit.
Filters tested and droppedRange expansion, volume, money flow, stochastic slope, stochastic level and the WGMI liquidity star all came back null or worse. The range expansion filter discarded a fifth of all diamonds and the discarded group had HIGHER upside.
IntradayThe diamond carries no information below the daily. On ETH 4H and 12H it scores −3 to +2pp at every horizon.
The 5D mechanismUnexplained. The same test with a BTC 5D confirmer shows nothing (p=1.00). Promising, not proven.

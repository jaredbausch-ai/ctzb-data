What each grade has done

A grade counts how many independent, tested lenses a signal has: C for none or one, B two, A three, A+ four or more. It sizes signals; it does not skip them. ETH lenses: CT more than 5 above its Signal line; entry above the previous signal; 2+ other ETH charts confirmed in the 10 days before; a BTC 3D signal in the 10 days before (1D and 2D); funding at or above its 90-day median; funding not cooling (7-day average at or above 14 days earlier). BTC lenses: CT more than 5 above its Signal line; 8D CT rising; an ETH 2D signal in the 10 days before (1D and 2D). The last four rows are flags shown beside the grade, not counted in it. Percentages are of signals; dips and upside are % from entry within 60 days. ETH from 2020 (when funding data starts), BTC from 2016.

What this is, and is not

  • Computed signals, not trades. Nothing here is a position taken. It is what the indicator flagged, measured against what price then did.
  • No exit is in any number. "+20% in 60d" means price touched that level, not that it was sold there. Upside is the best possible exit, not a return.
  • Odds, not certainty. A grade is a historical rate for that kind of setup. "Worst plausible" is the low end of a 95% range for the +20% rate.
  • Worst on record is not worst possible. Leave margin; do not size to the line.
  • The regime filter has a known blind spot. It stayed on through the November 2021 top, when signals 60 to 150 days into the regime went 3 for 35.
  • Entry is the close of the confirmation bar, three bars after the RSI low. Regime filter applied to Bitcoin 2D. Coinbase prices. Not financial advice.

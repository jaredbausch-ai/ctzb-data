Before we go through the package, here is what the signal actually produces
and what we can sell from it. Written for your AI. The business side is
yours; nothing here asks you to restructure it.

WHAT THE SIGNAL IS

A long-only entry signal on ETH and BTC. It marks a low in momentum and
confirms it a fixed number of bars later. The alert fires at that
confirmation close, so nothing is identified after the fact. It runs on the
1D, 2D, 3D, 5D and 8D charts. A Bitcoin-driven regime filter switches signals
on or off; it has been on since 2 September 2026. Each signal carries tags
(bucket A to D, diamond, prime dot) that place it in a historical group. The
method itself stays private.

WHAT EACH SIGNAL COMES WITH

- Chart, tag, entry price and time.
- The history of that exact setup: how many times it has printed, how often
  price reached +10%, +20% and +30% within 60 days, the typical drawdown below
  entry before the high, and the days it took.
- Context at that moment: whether other charts or the other asset signalled
  in the 10 days before, whether the higher timeframe is rising, and funding
  as a caution flag.

2022-26 is lower largely because the market moves less: ETH's daily

Narrower setups reach 70% to 90% but take far fewer trades. ETH 2D with the

ETH 2D with a BTC 3-day signal in the 10 days before: 77% (39 signals).
Smaller samples like these may not hold up going forward.

WHAT WE CAN SELL

1. Alerts at confirmation, with that setup's odds attached. The calculation
   already runs automatically every day.
2. Jared's live calls: when he buys and when he sells, by membership tier.

3. A record of every signal and every live call. Live calls can be logged

WHAT WE CANNOT CLAIM YET

- Profitability or returns. The figures above measure entries only; there is
  no exit in them. No position has been taken on any signal to date.
- Stop or invalidation levels. There is no mechanical exit; five tested exit
  rules all did worse than simply holding.
- Every figure here is a historical rate, not a forecast.

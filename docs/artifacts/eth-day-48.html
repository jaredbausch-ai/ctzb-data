What flips me

Both of these are visible on the board before price confirms them.

Bullish, early

The weekly diamond confirming — three weekly closes holding CT RSI above 52.88, entry around 8 November. That outranks everything else here. Second best is a 2D signal; that chart is clean right now, and 2D prime is 87/81/61 with a −1.8% median drawdown, the best risk-adjusted tag in the table.

Bearish

The weekly pivot failing to confirm — a close under ~$2,408 kills it before it exists. Then $2,000 going, or another lower-low cluster on 1D with nothing above it joining; 8D, 10D, 12D, 15D and 1M have all stayed silent since the regime turned on.

Where this could be wrong

  • n=5 and n=2. Five regime episodes, two clean cycle-low analogs. These are not distributions, they are anecdotes with arithmetic.
  • The 2016 tail is real. −48% from day 48. It was the DAO-fork crash, which is a one-off, but every fat left tail is a one-off until it isn't.
  • One unresolved data question. The engine calls diamonds on 1D at entry 5 Sep and 18 Sep that your chart's own plot columns don't flag. Until you eyeball those two bars, treat both as provisional.
  • Every forward number is measured on intrabar highs and lows, not closes. A level being "reached" means touched, not held.
